## Rex assessment (19 Aug 2026)

**Overall:** This is the best set of the entire project. The cumulative change from the Apr 15 baseline is now unmistakable across all five poses — and the 2-week change from the Aug 5 set is clearly visible. At 96 cm waist (−12 cm from baseline, −3 cm below Phase 1 end) and 75.6 kg (−9.4 kg from baseline), the cut has materially transformed the trunk composition.

**Front (01):** The single most striking change vs Aug 5. The lower belly is noticeably flatter — the belly button area no longer protrudes in the same way. The waist-to-chest taper is now well-established from the front; this is the pose that showed the least change early in the cut and is now showing the most continued improvement. The oblique line from ribcage to hip is emerging clearly.

**Left side — Baphomet sleeve (02):** The belly projection in full relaxed standing is at its lowest point in any photo set. The chest sits clearly and unambiguously forward of the midsection. The posterior chain and shoulder width look well-maintained — muscle retention is very good for 44 days of deficit. Compared to Aug 5, there's meaningful additional reduction in the lateral belly at waist level.

**Right side — mushroom (03):** The flattest profile recorded. The lower belly above the waistband still has soft tissue — this is the last area to go — but it's visibly reduced vs Aug 5 and dramatically reduced vs Phase 2 start. The side silhouette from chest to waist is now a clean line.

**Side relaxed (04):** The love handle / lateral waist is the most improved angle in this set compared to Aug 5. The oblique contour from armpit to hip has tightened noticeably. This angle, which showed the slowest early-phase progress, is now showing the most visible change in the final weeks.

**Arms raised (05):** Still the honest diagnostic. Lower belly soft tissue present under the arm-raise stretch — expected and appropriate at ~20% BF. The projection under the stretch is the smallest recorded across all sets. This is 44 days of real fat loss from the lower abdomen and love handles showing clearly.

**Key comparison vs Aug 5 (14 days ago):** The 2-week change is real and visible — 0.7 kg less fat + 1 cm waist in all five poses. The cuts in the final 11 days will continue this trajectory.

**What to expect from the final 11 days and refeed:** At ~20% BF with 11 days remaining, the final fat loss will be subtle on the scale but meaningful on the tape and in photos. The refeed (Sep 1–11) will fill out the muscles — these photos represent the "most depleted" state; Bali photos will show the same fat reduction but with fuller, more defined musculature.
