## Rex assessment (5 Aug 2026)

**Overall:** This is the clearest visual change set of Phase 2. The −2 cm waist in 14 days (99→97 cm) is reflected in all five poses. At 76.3 kg and 97 cm waist, this is now 2 cm below the Phase 1 end measurement (99 cm) at 2.7 kg lighter — confirming real fat loss, not just a lower scale number.

**Front (01):** The waist narrowing is now clearly visible. The belly button area sits noticeably flatter than both the Phase 2 start (Jul 6) and Week 3 (Jul 22) sets. The waist-to-shoulder taper is well-established. Lower abdomen soft tissue continues reducing but remains as the primary target for the final 4 weeks.

**Left side — sleeve (02):** The most diagnostic pose. Belly projection in full relaxed standing is meaningfully lower than the Jul 22 set. The belly sits clearly behind the chest line with more space between them than at any prior check-in. Posterior chain (upper back, lat sweep through the back sleeve) looks well-developed — strong muscle retention throughout the cut.

**Right side — mushroom (03):** Consistent improvement. The lower belly profile is the flattest it's been in any of the comparison sets. The oblique line from chest to hip is cleaner.

**Side relaxed (04):** Love handle reduction clearly visible vs Jul 22. The lateral waist is tightening from the side — the contour from armpit to hip is becoming more defined. This angle shows the week-on-week changes most clearly.

**Arms raised (05):** Still the honest diagnostic — lower belly soft tissue present under the arm-raise stretch, but the forward projection is substantially reduced from all previous sets. The belly no longer domes forward at this stretch. This is the pose that will show the most dramatic change in the final 4 weeks.

**Key comparison vs Week 3 (Jul 22):** The 2-week change is genuinely visible and meaningful. The combination of −1.6 kg on scale + −2 cm waist in 14 days represents real fat loss at the target rate. The cut is working exactly as designed.

**What to expect in the final 4 weeks (to Aug 30):** At the current 7d avg rate (~0.65 kg/wk), the scale will likely hit 74–75 kg by phase end. Waist may reach 94–95 cm. The lower belly and love handle — visible in pose 05 and 04 — are the remaining primary targets and will continue reducing meaningfully.
