**BF% working** (per the numerical reasoning standards — this is an estimate built on an estimate, treat the range as real):
Aug 19 set was assessed at ~20–22%; taking the 21% midpoint, 75.61 × 0.21 = **15.88 kg fat**. The 0.72 kg drop in 7-day average since then is treated as ~all fat, because the waist and the scale both say glycogen and water did *not* change across the refeed (see below), so there is no water swing to net out. 15.88 − 0.72 = **15.15 kg fat**, and 15.15 ÷ 74.89 = **20.2%**. Carry the ±2pp uncertainty from the Aug 19 figure through and the honest answer is **19–22%**, centred on 20%.

## Rex assessment (11 Sep 2026)

**Overall:** This is the leanest set of the project on every hard measure — 95 cm waist (−13 cm from baseline) and a 74.89 kg 7-day average (−10.4 kg from baseline), both the lowest recorded. But the honest visual read is that **the change from Aug 19 is modest**, and it is a slightly *leaner* version of that set rather than a *fuller* one. That distinction matters, and it is the main finding.

**The failed prediction is the story.** Aug 19 expected the refeed to fill the muscles out. It did not happen, and the photos agree with the data rather than with the forecast. The refeed was projected to add 1.5–2.0 kg of glycogen and bound water; it added **+0.09 kg across 14 days**, and the waist sat at 95 cm on 27 Aug, 3 Sep and again today — three readings, three weeks, no movement. So there is no glycogen-repletion effect to see in these images, and any impression of extra upper-body fullness should be attributed to lighting and pose, not to storage. This corroborates the 5 Sep `rebound_diagnosis_note` from an independent angle: the photos, the tape and the scale now all say the same thing.

**What that means is good news, not bad.** A 14-day refeed at true energy balance that produced no fat gain, no waist gain, and a 0.7 kg *reduction* in 7-day average is a clean outcome. The concern going into a refeed is always that "eat more" becomes fat regain; the tape ruling that out across three weeks is the single most reassuring number here.

**Front (01):** Chest sits forward of the midsection with a clear chest-to-waist taper, and the upper abdomen is flat. The lower belly below the navel still carries the soft tissue and the fold above the waistband — essentially unchanged in character from Aug 19. Shoulder and upper-arm development reads well. Versus Aug 19 the difference is marginal and mostly in how the chest catches the light.

**Left side — Baphomet sleeve (02):** Chest clearly forward of the belly. The line from chest to waist is clean and the back and shoulder look well maintained, which is the muscle-retention question answered positively across 56 days of Phase 2 deficit plus 14 days of balance. Lower belly projection above the waistband persists.

**Right side — mushroom (03):** The flattest profile recorded, marginally better than Aug 19. Lower belly above the waistband remains the dominant feature in profile. This is the last area to go and it is behaving exactly as expected at ~20%.

**Side relaxed (04):** The lateral waist and love-handle contour is where the cumulative Phase 2 change shows most against July. Against Aug 19 specifically it is close to unchanged.

**Arms raised (05):** Still the honest diagnostic, and still the least flattering — which is the point of keeping it. Lower-abdominal soft tissue is plainly present under the stretch. At ~20% BF this is expected and not a sign anything went wrong.

**Verdict against the phase's actual success criterion:** the criterion was never the kg — it was subjective recovery plus the waist holding at 95–96 cm. The waist held at 95 cm, the bottom of that band, and he reports feeling considerably better. **The phase passes on its own terms**, and it does so having landed within 0.11 kg of the revised 75.0 kg target by 7-day average.

**What to expect next:** Bali (12 Sep onward) will bring travel, restaurant food and higher sodium, so expect the scale to move 1–2 kg upward within days and the waist to read a little higher. On the evidence of this phase that will be water and gut content, not fat — do not re-diagnose it as regain. The next comparable set should be taken **after** returning and re-settling, under the same fasted morning conditions, or the comparison will be measuring travel rather than composition.
